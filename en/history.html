Common questions about border queues

When are border queues at their worst?

It depends on the crossing and the day, but summer Friday afternoons and Saturday mornings are typically the worst for leaving Serbia, while Sunday evening is the busiest for coming back in. The heatmap above shows the exact average by day and hour for each crossing.

What is the best day and time to drive to the coast?

Early on a weekday morning is almost always the clearest. Pick a crossing and a direction above to see the actual hourly average rather than a general rule of thumb.

Is the waiting pattern the same every week?

Not literally, but the pattern (busy Friday and Saturday, clear weekday mornings) holds through the summer season. The figures are averages of data actually measured at the border, not estimates.
