Serbia border crossings live: cameras and waiting times

Check live cameras and waiting estimates at Serbian border crossings towards Hungary, Croatia, Bosnia, Montenegro, Bulgaria, North Macedonia and Greece. Data combined from multiple sources: Serbian border police, the Hungarian and Macedonian sides, and driver reports.

Kako čitati brojke. Prikazujemo najgoru dostupnu procenu iz svih izvora za svaki smer, uz oznaku odakle je podatak. Kamere se vuku sa zvaničnih AMSS i MUP servera, pa video u nekim browserima možda neće da se učita (zavisi od CORS i referer politike izvora). Ako se ne učita, kartica nudi dugme ka zvaničnoj kameri. Kamera je uvek najpouzdanija provera stvarnog stanja.

Česta pitanja o granicama

Kolika je gužva na Horgošu ili Batrovcima sada?

Each crossing card shows a waiting estimate for entering and exiting Serbia, along with a live camera. For Horgoš and Kelebija we also have data from the Hungarian side, and for Preševo and Bogorodica from the North Macedonian side. The camera is the most reliable check of the current situation.

Where does the waiting data come from?

We combine multiple sources: the Serbian border police (via AMSS), the Hungarian police for crossings towards Hungary, the North Macedonian AMSM for crossings towards North Macedonia, and driver reports. For each direction we show the worst available estimate, with its source labelled.

Which crossing is faster, Horgoš or Kelebija?

Kelebija is a common alternative to Horgoš towards Hungary. Both cards show the current wait, and the trip calculator displays both crossings side by side for routes towards Hungary.

How do I watch a border camera live?

Click the play button on a crossing card. Cameras load directly from the official AMSS and MUP servers. If the video does not load in your browser, the card links to the official camera source.
