Bulgarian vignette 2026: prices, how to buy it and what if you don't

Updated: July 2026

If your route to Greece or Thessaloniki runs through Bulgaria (Dimitrovgrad/Gradina rather than Preševo), a vignette is mandatory on Bulgarian motorways — without one you risk a serious fine, and there is no grace period whatsoever. Here is exactly what you need to know, with verified 2026 prices.

What it costs

The Bulgarian vignette is time-based rather than distance-based — you pay for a period of validity, not for a stretch of road. For passenger vehicles up to 3.5 tonnes (category D1):

PeriodPriceWhen it makes sense
Daily (24h from activation)€4.09 (8 leva)Just passing through, one day
Weekend (Fri 12:00 – Sun 23:59)~€5 (10 leva)Short break that lines up with the weekend
Weekly (7 days)~€7.67A week's holiday
Monthly~€15.34Longer stay or several trips

For transit to Greece (a couple of hours of driving across Bulgaria) the daily vignette is plenty — there is no reason to pay for more.

No sticker — it is all electronic

Unlike Austria or Slovenia, the Bulgarian vignette is not a physical sticker for your windscreen. Since 2019 it has been fully electronic (e-vignette), tied directly to your vehicle's number plate. Enforcement is done by cameras that read plates on the motorway — nothing to stick on, but you must enter the plate number exactly right when buying.

How to buy one

  • Online, in advance — web.bgtoll.bg (the official site), paid by card or Apple/Google Pay, with confirmation by e-mail within minutes. You can buy up to 30 days ahead — just make sure you set the correct start date rather than the date of purchase.
  • At the border — self-service machines at the crossing, card only.
  • Just after the border — a kiosk on the left-hand side of the road as you enter Bulgaria, open 24h.
Keep the confirmation. You do not have to print it, but keep the PDF on your phone (a screenshot or the e-mail) — if you are stopped, that is your proof you are in the system.

If you are caught without one

The official fine for passenger vehicles is 300–3,000 leva (€150–1,500), but in practice there is a compensatory charge of €35.79 if you pay immediately on the spot (mobile patrols carry card terminals) or at the border before leaving the country. That is far cheaper than the full fine, which would otherwise arrive by post.

There is no grace period. Unlike Hungary (60 minutes) or Romania, in Bulgaria the fine applies even if you buy the vignette the moment you join the motorway, before you reach the first camera. Buy it before you enter, not after.

While we're at it — you don't need a green card here

Unlike the vignette, a green card is not required for Bulgaria (or for Greece) if you drive on Serbian plates — the multilateral agreement covers it. A green card is genuinely needed only for North Macedonia and Albania.

Common questions

How much does a Bulgarian vignette cost in 2026?

Daily €4.09, weekend ~€5, weekly ~€7.67, monthly ~€15.34 — for passenger vehicles up to 3.5 tonnes.

Do I need a physical sticker?

No. Everything has been electronic since 2019, tied to the number plate, with enforcement by camera.

What if I am caught without a vignette?

The official fine is €150–1,500, but the €35.79 compensatory charge paid on the spot is a far cheaper option. There is no grace period.
