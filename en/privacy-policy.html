Privacy policy

This page explains what data Gasolina collects when you use the site, why it is collected, who it is shared with, and how to switch it off.

Last updated: 27 July 2026

In short: Gasolina has no accounts, no sign-up and no passwords, and sells data to nobody. Anonymous traffic statistics are collected through Google Analytics, the site uses cookies (its own and Google's, including advertising ones), and whatever you type into the trip calculator is sent to a routing service so the route can be calculated. None of it is tied to your name.

1. Who runs the site

gasolina.rs is run by Gasolina, an independent private project based in Serbia. Gasolina is the data controller under Serbia's Personal Data Protection Act (Official Gazette RS no. 87/2018) and, for visitors from the EU, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, 2016/679).

Contact for any privacy question: kontakt@gasolina.rs

The site is static and published through GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which as the hosting provider processes technical access logs on its own account.

2. What data is collected

2.1 Automatically, when you open a page

The site uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-4EPLBJE8BL) for traffic statistics. On our behalf, Google Analytics processes:

  • a truncated IP address (Google Analytics 4 neither logs nor stores full IP addresses);
  • approximate location derived from the IP address — country, sometimes city;
  • device type, operating system, browser, screen resolution and language;
  • which pages you open, how long you spend on them, and the page you arrived from (referrer);
  • an anonymous browser identifier from a cookie, to tell a new visit from a returning one;
  • certain actions on the site (e.g. opening a camera, running the calculator) — without the content you typed.

Separately, GitHub Pages as the hosting provider keeps standard server logs (IP address, request time, requested path, user agent). We have no access to those logs.

2.2 Data you enter yourself

  • Trip calculator. The origin and destination you type, along with your consumption and fuel selections, are sent to OpenRouteService (HeiGIT gGmbH, Germany) to resolve the place names and calculate the route. Only the text you typed is sent, nothing that would identify you. The result is not stored on our side.
  • Email. If you write to kontakt@gasolina.rs, we keep your message and address for as long as it takes to reply and, where it is an error report, to fix the problem.

The site does not use your GPS location and never asks for device location permission.

2.3 What is not collected

  • no registration, accounts, passwords or user profiles;
  • no payments — we process no card data;
  • we do not collect your name, address, phone number or any special category of personal data;
  • data is not sold, rented or passed to data brokers.

6. Legal basis for processing

  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — keeping basic traffic statistics and keeping the site secure and working.
  • Performing the service you asked for — when you type an origin and destination, processing that input is necessary for the calculator to do what you asked of it.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — for analytics and personalised advertising cookies for visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. You give or refuse consent through the consent mechanism and may withdraw it at any time.

7. How long data is kept

  • Google Analytics — event data is kept for the retention period set on the Analytics property (Google's default is 14 months), after which it is deleted. Aggregated reports remain without individual records.
  • Cookies — until the lifetime shown in the table in section 3, or until you delete them.
  • Calculator inputs — not stored. There is no database for them to be written to.
  • Email correspondence — for as long as it takes to resolve the matter, normally no more than 12 months.
  • Hosting server logs — under GitHub's own terms.

8. International data transfers

Google (US and EU), GitHub (US) and Cloudflare (US) process data outside Serbia. Those transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. OpenRouteService runs on servers in Germany. Transfers out of Serbia are made in accordance with Articles 64–70 of the Personal Data Protection Act.

9. Your rights

In relation to data concerning you, you have the right to:

  • ask what data is processed and receive a copy of it;
  • ask for inaccurate data to be corrected;
  • ask for data to be deleted;
  • ask for processing to be restricted, and object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw consent you have given, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Send your request to kontakt@gasolina.rs and we will respond within 30 days. Note one practical limitation: because the site keeps no accounts, in most cases there is no way to link a visit record to a specific person — in that situation the fastest route is clearing cookies in your browser, after which that record no longer relates to you.

The supervisory authority in Serbia is the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection, Bulevar kralja Aleksandra 15, 11120 Belgrade, phone +381 11 3408 900, office@poverenik.rs, poverenik.rs. If you are in the EU, you may also complain to the supervisory authority in your own country.

10. How to opt out of tracking

  • Google Analytics — install the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on or block googletagmanager.com in your browser.
  • Personalised ads — adssettings.google.com, aboutads.info/choices, youronlinechoices.eu.
  • All cookies — your browser settings let you block third-party cookies or delete existing ones. The site keeps working; the only thing lost is the memory that you dismissed the install prompt.
  • Local storage and cache — clearing site data in your browser also clears gasolina_install_dismissed and the service worker cache.

11. Children

The site is aimed at drivers and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data about anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us their data, write to us and we will delete it.

12. Security

The entire site is served over HTTPS. There is no database, no user account and no server under our control where personal data could be written, which keeps the attack surface correspondingly small. No transmission of data over the internet is completely secure and we cannot guarantee otherwise.

13. External links and data accuracy

Fuel prices, border waiting times and camera stills are taken from public sources and shown for information only, with the source and timestamp stated. They may be out of date or wrong and are not official information. For decisions on the road, check with the relevant authority. The site links to other websites whose content and privacy practices we cannot influence.

14. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when what the site does changes — for example when a new data source or ad network is added. The date of the last change is at the top of this page. Significant changes will be announced on the home page.
